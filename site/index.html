NaNa

Your phone. In your words.

Say "nana", then a command. Speech recognition and text-to-speech run on your phone, offline. No internet permission. Nothing leaves the device.

Download APK View source

Android 10 or newer. Debug build for now.

What it does

Wake word "nana"

Say "nana" and then a command. Say "nana" alone and it opens an 8 second window for the next phrase.

Offline speech and voice

Vosk recognizes your speech on the device. The system offline text-to-speech talks back. No cloud, no account.

Foreground service

Listening keeps running when you switch apps, so you do not have to keep NaNa open.

Screen reading

With the accessibility service on, NaNa reads visible text, taps buttons by label, scrolls, and presses back, home, recents and notifications.

  • "nana open camera" or "launch" an app
  • "nana call" a number or a contact name
  • "nana press back", "go home", "recents", "open notifications"
  • "nana read screen", "scroll down", "tap" a button label
  • "nana what time is it", "help", "stop listening"

Build from source

Needs JDK 17 or 21 and the Android SDK with platform android-36.

  1. Clone github.com/Ahmadns7/nana.
  2. Put the Vosk model vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15 into app/src/main/assets/model. The model is not in the repo because of its size.
  3. Set sdk.dir in local.properties.
  4. Run ./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:assembleDebug.
  5. Install app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk.

Voice, wake word and screen reading are untested on a real phone so far. Test it and report back.
